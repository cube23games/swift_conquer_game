# Source Basis and Canon Boundaries

The master train preserves these recovered project decisions:

- Deterministic, headless simulation remains authoritative.
- Commands and validation precede rendering adapters.
- Ore mining, refineries, harvesters, and silos form the economy foundation.
- Starting funds default around 15,000 and remain configurable.
- The Mobile Headquarters Center deploys into an HQ.
- Owned buildings anchor an approximately two-to-three-tile build radius.
- Tap selection, re-tap deselection, one-finger box selection, and two-finger
  panning remain the mobile-control direction.
- Up to three engineers contribute to maximum building-repair speed.
- Manual repair does not drain power and does not stack with engineer repair.
- Low power slows engineer repair, production, and aircraft reloading.
- Aircraft remain operable during low power.
- Superweapons stop under low power.
- All factions must remain viable on land and able to build water units.
- The baseline scale target is about 100 units per side, with an optional
  high-end target around 200.
- Spy access is data-driven; selling the source building for ten percent of
  face value removes stolen technology access.
- AI, victory, replay verification, and integrity checks are implemented before
  the final thin UI adapter.

The recovered material did not provide canonical faction names, the exact full
rosters, final technology mappings, final market percentages, or final balance
formulas. Those values remain explicit data placeholders and are not invented
inside this train.
